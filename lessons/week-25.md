# Week 25: English, Applications and Mock Interviews

## Goal

Speak clearly about technical work in English and run a steady application
pipeline.

## Part 1: interview English

30% of the Bangladesh postings ask for English explicitly; every overseas
and remote role requires it. You do not need a perfect accent. You need to
be **clear, structured and unhurried**.

### Habits

1. **Slow down.** Speed is the main cause of unclear speech.
2. **Short sentences.** One idea each.
3. **Signpost.** "There are three parts. First... Second... Third..."
4. **Answer first, then explain.** "Yes, we used a cache. The reason was..."
5. **Check understanding.** "Should I go deeper into the retrieval part?"
6. **Ask when unsure.** "Could you repeat the last part?" is normal.
7. **Pause instead of filling.** Silence sounds more confident than "um".

### Useful phrases

| Situation | Phrase |
| --- | --- |
| Buying thinking time | "Let me think about that for a moment." |
| Clarifying | "Just to confirm, are you asking about X or Y?" |
| Structuring | "I'd approach this in three steps." |
| Stating an assumption | "I'll assume about a thousand requests a day; tell me if that's off." |
| Trade-off | "The trade-off is higher accuracy for more latency." |
| Not knowing | "I haven't used that in production. My understanding is..." |
| Disagreeing politely | "I see it a bit differently, because..." |
| Recovering | "Let me correct that. What I meant was..." |
| Closing | "That's my overall design. Which part would you like to explore?" |

### Vocabulary to pronounce correctly

Retrieval, embedding, latency, throughput, idempotent, asynchronous, cache,
queue, schema, architecture, inference, quantization, hallucination,
hierarchy, parameter, algorithm, authentication, authorization.

Look each up in an online dictionary with audio and repeat it.

### Daily practice (30 minutes)

- 10 minutes: explain one concept aloud, recorded (use the week 24 list).
- 10 minutes: listen to a technical talk or podcast; shadow one minute of it
  (repeat right after the speaker).
- 10 minutes: write a short technical paragraph; read it aloud.

Weekly: one full mock interview in English, recorded and reviewed.

## Part 2: the application pipeline

### Targets by experience (you will have about 2 years)

| Tier | Roles | Share of effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core | Bangladesh AI and LLM engineer roles asking 1 to 3 years | 60% |
| Stretch | Bangladesh roles asking 3 years; remote contracts | 30% |
| Long shot | Overseas roles with relocation | 10% |

### Materials

- CV: one page, AI work first, numbers, links to the four projects.
- LinkedIn: headline, About section and Featured projects match the CV.
- GitHub: four pinned projects with complete READMEs.
- A short cover note template, three sentences, customised per company:
  what they build, the one project of yours that matches, a link.

### Tailor for each application (10 minutes)

1. Read the posting; list its five main requirements.
2. Reorder your skills line so those appear first.
3. Make sure a bullet or project shows each one you have.
4. Use their wording where it is true for you (for example "agentic
   workflows", "LLMOps").

### Where to find roles

- LinkedIn job alerts for "AI Engineer", "LLM Engineer", "Generative AI",
  "Agentic AI", "Machine Learning Engineer", location Bangladesh and Remote.
- Bdjobs and company career pages (the report found many roles posted only
  there).
- Referrals: message engineers at target companies with a specific question
  about their work, not a request for a job. Referrals convert far better
  than cold applications.

### Track everything

A spreadsheet: company, role, link, date applied, contact, stage, next step,
notes. Follow up once after 7 to 10 days.

### Weekly rhythm

| Activity | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Tailored applications | 8 to 10 |
| Referral conversations | 3 |
| Mock interviews | 1 to 2 |
| Public post or project update | 1 |

Expect a low response rate. Rejections without an interview mean the CV
needs work; rejections after interviews mean practise that round.

## Part 3: mock interviews

2. Coding (week 23).
3. Project walkthrough plus behavioural (week 24).

After each, write three things that went well and three to fix. Fix one per
day.
